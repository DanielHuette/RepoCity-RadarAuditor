RepoCity RadarAuditor

RadarAuditor measures how visible a business is on Google, on the map, in directories and in AI assistants — and what is missing.

For roofers, dentists, kitchen studios and every business whose customers come from nearby. The first audit runs from the outside and needs no access to your accounts.

What the customer gets

Three things, all from measurements of the business itself and of up to three competitors nearby.

A score from 0 to 100, with a traffic light per area

Green from 75, amber from 50, red below. At a glance it is clear whether the Google profile, the website or the reviews hold the business back.

Findings in plain language, with a ready fix

Each finding says what was measured, what it costs and what to do. When the fix is a text or a piece of code, it comes ready to copy.

A forecast as a range, explicitly an estimate

How many more enquiries are possible is shown as a range from low to high, with every assumption listed underneath. It is not a promise.

How an audit runs

A job goes round once. Scoring follows fixed rules; writing happens only at the end. Depending on the size of the website it takes a few minutes to half an hour.

  1. App on the phoneThe operator enters only the website address, picks the checks and starts the remote audit. The server works out the business, town, industry and search terms itself.
  2. SupabaseThe job lands in the database queue. If a step fails, the server tries it again.
  3. Collector containerRuns on the RepoCity server. First identifies the business, town, industry and search terms from the website and the Google profile, finds up to three competitors on the map, then fetches the website, load time, Google profile, positions in Google and Bing search, directories and the answers of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity.
  4. ScoringEach of the 108 checks is passed, partly passed, failed or not measurable; deselected checks do not count. Code computes the points, not a language model.
  5. Claude CodeRuns in the collector container, signed in with the Claude subscription. Reads the raw data and check results and writes one finding with a fix per open point, plus the report texts and a talking script for the customer. Before anything is uploaded, the collector validates the result against a fixed data format.
  6. Report as PDFThe collector assembles the report as a print-ready file. It appears in the app and can be walked through with the customer on a tablet.

Thirteen areas, one number

The score is a weighted average of thirteen areas. Weights, checks and thresholds live in a single check catalogue; the weights add up to 100. A fourteenth area shows calls, clicks and enquiries when the customer grants access and does not count towards the score.

What is measured, and what is not

A lot can be measured from the outside, but not everything. What is missing appears in the report as “not measurable” and does not count towards the points, instead of being guessed.

Measurable from the outside, no access needed

  • Encryption, load time on phone and PC, layout on the phone
  • Page titles, headings, texts, broken links
  • Name, address and phone on the website compared with Google, Apple Maps, Bing and business directories
  • Machine-readable company data for Google and AI assistants
  • Google profile: categories, opening hours, photos, number and average of reviews
  • Positions in Google and Bing search and on the map, including a grid around the location, compared with the competitors
  • Whether ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity name the business, and whether what they say is correct
  • Legal notice and privacy policy

Only with customer access or paid data

  • Calls, route requests, clicks: figures from the Google profile, Search Console and Analytics are only available with access.
  • Replies to reviews: the server reads them from Google Maps in the browser; if Maps blocks the browser they are missing, and an audit with access reads them from the profile.
  • New reviews per month: Google Maps gives the date of a review only roughly, such as “2 weeks ago”; if Maps blocks the browser, Google only returns the five newest publicly. So the figure is an estimate.
  • Description, services, products, posts, questions and answers, service areas and attributes of the Google profile: only visible in Google Maps in the browser; if Maps blocks the browser they are missing for that audit.
  • Links from other sites, search volume, competitors’ search terms, visibility trend: only with a paid data service with a monthly limit; once it is reached these checks are “not measurable”.
  • Positions in Google search: searching in the browser can hit a block page; then the server fetches the missing positions later, or at once with the paid data service.

Before and after the audit

The app finds businesses that would benefit from an audit, gives undecided customers real reply drafts to take away and prepares the work for every month of a subscription. What is promised is this work, never a particular position on Google.

  1. Finding customersIn the “Kunden” (customers) tab the operator enters a trade and a town, for example roofers in Münster. The server reads up to 60 businesses from Google Maps and sorts them by gaps in their Google profile, each with one sentence saying why: no website, no opening hours, no phone number, few photos or clearly fewer reviews than half of the list.
  2. Add or audit right away“Ins CRM als Interessent” (add to the CRM as a prospect) creates the business in the customer relationship management system (CRM) with an open case. Only what the operator copies from the public Google profile is stored. “Audit starten” (start audit) checks the business's website just like the “Prüfen” (audit) tab.
  3. Only permitted ways of contactEach business shows the ways that section 7 of the German Act against Unfair Competition allows: letter and personal visit, a phone call only with a concrete reason, an advertising email without consent never. That is why there is neither an email nor a dial button. Anyone who has objected to advertising is on a block list and appears without any way of contact.
  4. Costs and storage time cappedThe customer search has its own daily limit and a monthly limit in euros, so the audits never run short. After 24 hours the database deletes the list by itself, even when the server is off.
  5. Reply drafts as a PDFWhen a customer says “let me think about it”, the server writes up to five replies to their real reviews that have no reply yet and presents them as a PDF. Nothing is published on Google.
  6. Monthly serviceOnce the first optimisation is finished, drafts are prepared after every monthly audit of a subscription: replies to new reviews and suggestions for looking after the profile, such as opening hours, photos, posts and description, never a change to the website. The operator approves each draft on its own.

Who it is for

Businesses

The remote audit needs nothing from you: no access, no password, no appointment. For ongoing care you grant access to your Google profile once and, if you use it, to Search Console. Following the guide this takes about five minutes, and your password stays with you. After that a report arrives every month showing what has changed, together with drafts of replies to new reviews and of care for your profile, which are only carried out once approved. What is promised is this work, not a particular position on Google.

Request an audit: kontakt@speedofthespirit.dev

Companies

One check catalogue is the single source for checks, weights and thresholds; code, app and texts derive from it. Code computes the points by fixed rules; the language model only writes the texts and takes every number from the measured data. What Claude Code returns is validated against the data model, and invalid results stay behind.

An order in RadarAuditor, from the app to the invoice

From the website address typed on a phone to the invoice in the CRM: eight stations that hand work to each other. Each station lists the skills at work there.

  1. App on phone, tablet and PC

    The operator enters only the website address, picks the checks and starts the remote audit. Open audit requests from the CRM are listed below the “Prüfen” (audit) button; a request with a website fills in the address field. In the “Kunden” (customers) tab the operator finds the businesses of one trade in one town.

    Cloudflare Pages

    • Installable web app (Progressive Web App)
    • Preact and TypeScript
    • Charts with Chart.js
    • Open requests: crm.open_audit_requests

  2. Queue in Supabase

    The order waits as a job in the database. When a step fails, the server retries it up to three times.

    Supabase, hosted in Frankfurt

    • PostgreSQL with migrations
    • Row level security
    • File storage for raw data and reports

  3. Collector on the server

    Identifies business, city and industry by itself and fetches the website, load time, Google profile, search positions, directories and answers from AI assistants.

    Docker container on the RepoCity server

    • Python
    • Browser automation with Playwright
    • httpx and BeautifulSoup

  4. Scoring by fixed rules

    108 checks from a single check catalogue. Code computes the points, not a language model.

    in the collector container

    • Check catalogue as a YAML file
    • Data models with Pydantic
    • Tests with pytest

  5. Evaluation with Claude Code

    Writes a finding with a fix for every open point, plus the report texts. It may only read and write two files; the result is validated against a fixed data format.

    in the collector container, on the Claude subscription

    • Headless Claude Code with the skill /auswerten
    • Permissions cut to the minimum
    • Output validated against a schema

  6. Report as PDF

    Print-ready report with charts and a forecast as a range; for the monthly audit a before-after report. On request also up to five reply drafts to real reviews.

    in the collector container

    • PDF with WeasyPrint
    • Templates with Jinja2
    • Charts with Matplotlib

  7. Quote on site

    Positions from the open points of the audit, products and prices from the CRM. Audit report and quote leave as one PDF through the CRM.

    App, CRM database

    • CRM database functions instead of a separate interface
    • Quote and sending: crm.create_quote, crm.send_quote

  8. CRM and Cockpit

    Acceptance creates the order. Claude Code drafts changes to website and Google profile, the collector applies the approved ones. Then the CRM completes the order and sends the invoice.

    RepoCity CRM and Cockpit

    • Claude Code with the skill /optimieren
    • Database trigger reports “order finished”
    • Claude Code reads reply mails without any tools

Customer search Trade and town in the “Kunden” (customers) tab: the server reads up to 60 businesses from Google Maps, sorts them by gaps in their Google profile and names the permitted ways of contact for each, letter and visit. “Ins CRM als Interessent” (add to the CRM as a prospect) creates the business in the CRM, “Audit starten” (start audit) puts the remote audit of its website into the queue. Businesses on the block list for objections to advertising appear without any way of contact and without a button.

Audit request An audit request in the CRM names the business, the meeting type (on site, phone or video) and a free hour from Daniel's calendar. In the Cockpit it arrives with “Bestätigen” (confirm), “Anderen Termin vorschlagen” (propose another time) and “Prüfen” (audit); “Prüfen” puts the remote audit of its website into the queue.

Monthly audit Once a month the CRM marks a subscription due. One tap in the Cockpit puts the order back into the queue, and the report then shows before and after. Then the collector prepares the monthly service: drafts of replies to new reviews and of profile care, which the operator approves one by one.

Every change to the code runs through the same checks with GitHub Actions: formatting and style with ruff, types with mypy, tests with pytest, plus check, tests and build of the app.
